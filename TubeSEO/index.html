FAQ

Quick answers

Is TubeSEO free?

Yes. All core tools — tag generator, tag extractor, keyword tool, hashtag generator, video analyzer, channel analyzer, thumbnail downloader, trending, and revenue estimator — are free to use. A small banner ad keeps the lights on.

Do I need to log in?

No login, no account. Just install and start. We don't collect any creator credentials.

Does it work offline?

Mostly. Every video and channel you look up is cached, so you can keep browsing recent results without a connection. Fresh trending data and new lookups need internet.

What platforms is it on?

TubeSEO is available on both platforms — Android on Google Play and iOS on the App Store. Download it on whichever device you prefer.

Is the revenue estimate accurate?

It's an informed estimate based on view counts and typical creator CPM ranges. Actual earnings depend on geography, audience demographics, advertiser demand, and platform policy, so treat the number as a planning ball-park, not a guarantee.

Is TubeSEO affiliated with YouTube?

No. TubeSEO is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google LLC. YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC. TubeSEO uses publicly available information and official APIs where applicable.

How do I contact support?

Email support@yubecalc.com — we read everything and reply within a few business days.
